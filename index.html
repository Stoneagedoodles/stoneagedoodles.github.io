About

Stone Age Studio is a small private tool used by the creator of the stick-figure history channel Stone Age Doodles (YouTube, Instagram and TikTok: @stoneagedoodles).

It runs only on the creator's own computer and is used to plan, upload and review the creator's own videos on the creator's own accounts. It is not offered to other users.

Contact: send a direct message to @stoneagedoodles on TikTok or Instagram.
